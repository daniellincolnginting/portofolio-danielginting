Project Overview

Problem

Healthcare providers like BPJS Kesehatan serve millions of members, generating massive amounts of unstructured public feedback on social media. Manually analyzing this data to pinpoint exactly what the public is complaining about (e.g., app errors vs. hospital queues) and how they feel about it is highly inefficient and prone to human bias.

Objective

Develop an automated, data-driven NLP model capable of extracting specific discussion topics (Service Quality dimensions) and accurately classifying public sentiment from noisy Indonesian social media text.

Approach at a glance

  • Data: over 5,000 public comments about BPJS Kesehatan services, collected from social media X and Instagram.
  • Topics: BERTopic was compared with a rule-based approach to group comments into 5 main topics aligned with the SERVQUAL service quality dimensions.
  • Sentiment: IndoBERT was fine-tuned with a hybrid labeling strategy to classify sentiment in Indonesian text.
  • Validation: the output was checked against expert judgment to confirm it holds up in practice.
  • Why it matters: the results show which service aspects people talk about and how they feel, so improvements can be prioritized with data instead of guesswork.

Result Summary of The Project

Topic Modeling Optimization

Successfully compared an unsupervised machine learning approach (BERTopic) against a Rule-Based approach to map unstructured public comments into distinct Service Quality (ServQual) dimensions such as Reliability and Responsiveness.

High-Performance Sentiment Classification

Fine-tuned the IndoBERT deep learning architecture utilizing a hybrid labeling strategy, achieving robust evaluation metrics (Accuracy and F1-Score) specifically optimized for the Indonesian language structure.

Expert Validation Alignment

The model’s output was rigorously validated against Expert Judgment, proving the pipeline’s real-world reliability in extracting actionable insights from public healthcare feedback.
